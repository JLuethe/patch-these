# 25 November 2026

Coverage: 23–25 November 2026.

Issues raised and discussed on X, checked against vendor advisories and, where the day's sources gave a figure, the CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog and FIRST EPSS. X posts are linked, not copied. This digest does not include exploit code, proof-of-concept code, or attack steps.

Scores appear only when a source for this digest stated them. A missing score was not filled in from anywhere else.

Patch now means actively exploited, or critical and widely deployed. Patch soon means high severity with a patch. Watch means no patch, or limited impact.

## Applications & servers

### Drupal core

**Patch now**

Drupal rates CVE-2026-132165 Highly critical. It is actively exploited, and CISA added it to the KEV catalog on 24 November 2026. Abuse of the installer can lead to a site takeover. End-of-life lines are not fixed.

Update to 10.6.22, 11.3.22, or 11.4.13. Until you are on one of those, lock down `/core/install.php` and keep `settings.php` unwritable.

Discussed on X: [itsMikeKipruto](https://x.com/itsMikeKipruto/status/2125561622307500255)

Official: [Drupal SA-CORE-2026-017](https://www.drupal.org/sa-core-2026-017) · [CISA KEV alert, 24 November](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/11/24/cisa-adds-three-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)

### VMware vCenter

**Patch now**

Authentication bypass, CVSS 9.8. CVE-2026-123890 is an exploited zero-day, added to the KEV catalog on 20 November 2026 (EPSS 5.01%). CVE-2026-123903 was published on 24 November 2026. A public proof of concept has been reported for it, and it is not known to be exploited. This digest does not describe or link that proof of concept.

Update vCenter to 9.1.2, 9.0.2.0300, or 8.0 U3m, and VCF 5.2.5. There is no workaround. Keep vCenter off the internet.

Discussed on X: [securestep9](https://x.com/securestep9/status/2125247521727373445)

Official: [Broadcom security advisory](https://support.broadcom.com/web/ecx/support-content-notification/-/external/content/SecurityAdvisories/0/39626)

## Patch these

### For app and server owners

#### Patch now

- **Chrome, including Electron apps that ship their own copy.** Install Chrome 158.0.8127.88 on Linux, or 158.0.8127.88 or 158.0.8127.89 on Windows and Mac. CVE-2026-134401, a sandbox escape via a crafted page, is not known to be exploited. The update also covers exploited CVE-2026-125350, which was fixed in 157.0.8103.50.
