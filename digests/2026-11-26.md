CVE-2026-102268 is a key-confusion issue in PyJWT when an application accepts both HMAC and asymmetric algorithms. PyJWT before 2.14.0 is affected, and NVD records the fix as 2.14.0. The project release page at https://github.com/jpadilla/pyjwt/releases/tag/2.14.0 was not opened. Social posts mention Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform 2.5; the Red Hat fix status was not established. Exploitation was not established, and the CVE was not in the CISA KEV catalog at this check.

**What to do:** Update PyJWT to 2.14.0 where the application accepts both HMAC and asymmetric algorithms. This edition does not establish a fixed release for Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform.

**Why:** The PyJWT fix has been out since September, and only applications that mix HMAC and asymmetric algorithm families are exposed.

**Applies to:** PyJWT before 2.14.0, when the application accepts both HMAC and asymmetric algorithms. Social posts mention Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform 2.5; that product's fix status was not established.

**CVEs:** CVE-2026-102268

**Fixes by component / platform / branch:**

- PyJWT: ` 2.14.0 `; CVE-2026-102268.

**Exploitation:** CVE-2026-102268 unknown.
- KEV CVE-2026-102268: not listed.

### 1Password

**Mitigate · My computer**

1Password says an attacker used an employee's GitHub account, starting 2026-11-19, to push code to backend services and copied account records for a subset of customers. The copied data includes names, email addresses, identifiers, sign-in times, and some tokens in emailed invitation or recovery links. Vaults, passwords, and Secret Keys were not accessed. There is no CVE. CVSS, EPSS, and KEV do not apply.

**What to do:** No account change is needed, per 1Password. Be wary of unexpected 1Password email, including invitations or recovery requests you did not start.

**Why:** There is no patch for customers to apply. The remaining risk is unexpected invitation or recovery email.

**Applies to:** A subset of 1Password customers. Vaults, passwords, and Secret Keys were not accessed.

**Exploitation:** confirmed.
